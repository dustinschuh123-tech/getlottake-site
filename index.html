Quick Tour or Full Tour

Thirty seconds they'll actually watch. Or the full two-minute tour.

Quick Tour is the one a busy customer finishes: the look, what's under the hood, the best of the interior and one fun fact, in about 30 seconds.

Full Tour is for the customer who's almost ready. It walks the outside, then the cabin seat by seat, with the power, tech, safety and fun facts LotTake found on that VIN, in about two minutes.

Either way it sounds like you walking the car, not a spec sheet: outside first, then under the hood, then inside, with no repeats. Want it your way? Hand-pick every line.
